JORDAN · 02

The product

Mira

A spec-first agent for ops. Takes a written runbook, executes it, asks when stuck.

JORDAN · 03

What worked

Three bets that held.

  • Narrow scope. We do ops. Not chat, not analysis, not creative.
  • Spec-first. Every action cites a runbook line. No spec, no execution.
  • Eval gates. PRs that drop replay coverage don't merge.
JORDAN · 04

What broke

And what we're still fixing.

  • Context limits on tasks past ~3 hours of tool history
  • Hallucinated tools when the runbook was vague
  • Tail latency from sequential calls — moved to parallel where safe
